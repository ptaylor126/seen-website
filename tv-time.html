Moving from TV Time: bring your history into Seen

If you have an export of your TV Time data made with TV Time Liberator, you can bring your shows, watched episodes and the dates you watched them into Seen, a free app for iPhone and Android.

What you need

One of these files:

  • The export from TV Time Liberator (tv-time-export.zip), or the shows.json, movies.json or activity_history.csv inside it.
  • Files from the TV Time converter tool (tvtime-series-<date>.json and tvtime-movies-<date>.json).

Not supported yet: TV Time's own "download my data" file. Seen recognises it but can't read it yet.

How to import

  1. Download Seen and sign up. You're offered an import during sign-up, or you can do it later.
  2. Go to Profile, open Settings and choose Import your library.
  3. Choose TV Time and pick your file.
  4. Check what Seen found, then import.

What comes across

  • Your shows, with every watched episode and the date you watched it.
  • Your films and film ratings.
  • Your watchlist.

Shows you're part-way through land as Watching, shows you finished that have ended land as Watched, and shows you followed but hadn't started land on your Watchlist.

You see everything before it's added. Running the import again doesn't create duplicates, and anything already in Seen stays as it is.

What doesn't come across

  • Episode ratings. Seen rates whole films and shows, not single episodes.
  • Old TV Time comments. Episode comments in Seen start fresh.
  • Some episodes that TV Time numbered differently, such as some anime and specials. Seen lists these rather than guessing.

After you import

Seen tracks shows episode by episode, sends a notification the morning after a new episode of a show you're caught up on comes out, and lets you send recommendations to friends with a note.

Seen isn't affiliated with TV Time or TV Time Liberator.

Questions

I didn't export my TV Time data. Can I still use Seen?

Yes. You can add shows by hand, mark a whole season watched, or tell Seen which episode you're up to. You can also paste a plain list of titles to import.

Is Seen free?

Yes. Seen is free on iPhone and Android.
