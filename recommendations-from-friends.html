The best app for film and TV recommendations from friends

Seen is a free app for iPhone and Android built around recommendations from friends. You send a film or show to a friend with a note saying why, and it stays in the app, so you never lose who recommended what.

Why friends, not an algorithm

Most people already find what to watch through friends. The trouble is that those recommendations get lost in group chats and notes apps. Seen keeps them in one place, with the friend's name and their note on each one.

How it works

  • Send a rec with a note. Pick a film or show, pick one or more friends, and say why they'd like it.
  • Send it to someone who isn't on Seen yet. Share a link by message or email. When they join from that link, your rec is waiting for them and you become friends.
  • React and talk about it. Friends can react to a rec and comment on it.
  • Ask a friend for a rec. Stuck for something to watch? Ask.
  • See what friends are watching. The Friends tab shows what your friends are watching and what they thought.
  • See who you agree with. Seen shows which friends rate things most like you do.

Picking something together

Tonight lets you swipe through ideas from your recs and your watchlist. Do it with a friend or partner, and Seen shows the ones you both said yes to.

Your friends first

On every film and show page, your friends' ratings and reviews come first. Seen also tracks everything you watch, films and TV, with episode tracking, ratings and a diary.

Questions

Do my friends need the app?

To see recs in the app, yes. But you can send a rec to anyone by link, and when they join Seen from it, the rec is waiting for them.

Is Seen free?

Yes. Seen is free on iPhone and Android.

Can I keep some things private?

Yes. Each title has a Visible to friends switch, so you can keep anything to yourself.
