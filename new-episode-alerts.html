How to get notified when new episodes come out

Use Seen, a free app for iPhone and Android. Tick off episodes as you watch, and when you're caught up on a show, Seen sends you a notification the morning after a new episode comes out.

How it works

  1. Add the show and mark it Watching. Search for it in Seen and tap Watching.
  2. Tick off episodes as you go. Tick one episode, a whole season, or tell Seen you're up to, say, season 3 episode 4.
  3. Turn on notifications. When a new episode of a show you're caught up on comes out, you get one notification at 10am your time the next morning.

No flood of alerts. You get at most one new-episode notification a day, and only for shows you're caught up on. You can turn them off in Settings.

See what's coming up

The Upcoming list shows new episodes on the way for the shows you're watching. Tap a show to jump straight to your next episode.

On the home screen, Up next shows the next episode of each show you're part-way through, and you can tick it off with one tap.

Where to watch it

Each show and episode page lists the streaming services that have it, and where you can rent or buy it.

Films too

Seen tracks films as well as TV, with a watchlist, half-star ratings and a diary of everything you've watched. You can also send recommendations to friends, with a note.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. Seen is free on iPhone and Android.

Can I bring my shows from another app?

Yes. Seen can import your history from TV Time (using a TV Time Liberator export), Letterboxd and IMDb, and from a plain list you paste in.
