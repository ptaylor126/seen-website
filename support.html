Common questions

How do I add a friend?

Go to the Friends tab, tap Add, and search for your friend by their handle. Send a request, and once they accept, you'll be connected.

How do I recommend something to a friend?

Open any film or show, choose to recommend it, pick the friend or friends you want to send it to, and add a note. It will arrive in their inbox.

Can I send a rec to someone who isn't on Seen?

Yes. When you recommend something, choose Send to someone not on Seen and share the link however you like. When they join Seen from that link, your rec is waiting for them and you become friends.

How do I import my library from another app?

Go to the Profile tab, open Settings, and choose Import your library. You're also offered it when you first sign up. Pick TV Time, Letterboxd or IMDb and follow the steps on screen. From TV Time, you'll need the export you made with TV Time Liberator (tv-time-export.zip). From Letterboxd, use the data export from your account settings. From IMDb, export your ratings or your watchlist as a CSV from the imdb.com website. Anything already in your Seen library stays as it is.

Who can see my episode comments?

Episode comments and reactions are public, but only to people on Seen who have ticked that episode as watched. Until you've ticked an episode, other people's comments on it stay hidden, so nobody spoils it for you. Your friends' comments show first. Comments are up to 500 characters, with likes. There are no replies, and links aren't allowed. Who liked a comment is never shown.

Can other people see what I've watched?

Only your friends can see your library, and never the titles you've marked private. Title and episode pages show how many people on Seen watched them, for example "30 people on Seen watched this". That's only a count, rounded down, and never says who. It only appears once at least 5 people have watched, and titles you've marked private aren't counted.

Can I keep something private?

Yes. You can mark any title in your library as private, and it won't appear on your profile or to your friends.

How do I report something?

Tap the menu on any comment, message, note, review or profile and choose Report. We review every report.

How do I block someone?

You can block someone from their profile. Blocking hides your content from each other and ends any friendship between you.

How do I complain or appeal a decision?

Email hello@seenrecs.com about any moderation decision or report. We reply within 14 days.

How do I delete my account?

Go to the Profile tab, open Settings, then Account (under Privacy and account), and choose Delete account. This permanently deletes your account and your data, and cannot be undone. Recommendations you've sent stay in your friends' inboxes with your name removed, and your handle is held for 90 days so nobody else can claim it. More about deleting your account.
