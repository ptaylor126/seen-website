A Letterboxd for TV shows

Seen is a free app for iPhone and Android that tracks films and TV shows in one place. You can log, rate and review TV the way you do films, track shows episode by episode, and keep a diary of when you watched everything.

Films and TV in one app

  • Watchlist, Watching, Watched. Every film and show sits in one library.
  • Half-star ratings and reviews for films and shows.
  • Episode by episode. Tick off episodes or whole seasons, and pick up where you left off with Up next.
  • A diary of everything you've watched, by date.
  • Episode comments that stay hidden until you've ticked the episode, so nobody spoils it.
  • Your Top 5 films and shows, and stats on what you've watched this month and this year.
  • New-episode alerts the morning after a new episode of a show you're caught up on comes out.

Bring your Letterboxd history

Seen can import your Letterboxd data export: your films, ratings, watchlist and the dates you watched them. You can also import from IMDb and from TV Time (using a TV Time Liberator export).

Built around friends

The difference with Seen is that it's built around people you know. You send recommendations to friends with a note, see what they're watching, and see their ratings first on every title.

Seen isn't affiliated with Letterboxd.

Questions

Can I import from Letterboxd?

Yes. Export your data from your Letterboxd account settings, then in Seen go to Profile, Settings, Import your library, and choose Letterboxd.

Is Seen free?

Yes. Seen is free on iPhone and Android.
